# Fedgix Restaurant OS — feature overview

A restaurant operating platform connecting customer ordering, floor service, kitchen preparation, billing, stock and management reporting.

## Demonstrated functionality

- Branded mobile menu, customer accounts, personal order history, collection orders and table-booking requests.
- Waiter mobile order entry, additions, table transfer and kitchen progress.
- Kitchen tickets, preparation states, notes and delayed-order visibility.
- Server-calculated bills, authorized discounts, manual payment records, split tender, partial payments and refunds.
- Ingredient/recipe stock tracking, supplier deliveries and wastage.
- Staff accounts, attendance, shifts, leave approval and basic payroll with private salary statements.
- Owner operations overview and existing accounting/reporting tools.

## Scope still to complete

Reservation party-size changes and linked meal preorders; separate split invoices; full payroll allowances/statutory rules/payment tracking; customer recovery/complaint flows; closed-day financial policy and employee branch isolation. Live payment settlement, messaging delivery and physical printers have not been verified. Production acceptance and hosting remain pending.
